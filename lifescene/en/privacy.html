LifeScene — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

LifeScene (the “App”) is designed so you can organize and revisit your photos and memories with peace of mind. This Privacy Policy explains how user information is handled in the App.

1. Access to photos and videos

The App accesses your photo library to create Scenes, display photos and videos, and detect on-device periods that may correspond to memories.

The App does not send your photos or videos themselves to a LifeScene-owned server, and does not duplicate them for storage inside the App.

2. Information used on the device

The App primarily uses the following information on your device:

  • Capture date and time of photos and videos
  • Media type and video duration
  • Location information (when present in a photo)
  • Favorites, screenshots, bursts, and similar metadata
  • Identifiers used to recognize photos and videos in the photo library

This information is used to provide App features such as displaying Scenes and detecting memory candidates on the device.

3. Information stored on the device

The App may store the following information on your device:

  • Scene titles
  • Scene date ranges
  • Tags
  • Information identifying the cover photo
  • Information identifying hidden photos or videos
  • Scene creation dates
  • Settings needed to use the App (onboarding, auto-detection state, and similar)

The photo and video data themselves are not stored as App-owned media files.

4. Data sent off the device

The App does not send your photos, videos, Scene information, or similar data to a LifeScene-owned server.

The App also does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

5. Face ID and device authentication

The App may use Face ID or device authentication to verify your identity, for example when exiting a display mode intended for showing photos to others.

The App does not obtain or store your biometric data itself.

6. Accounts

The App does not require account registration or sign-in.

7. Services provided by Apple

The App uses standard iOS features such as the photo library and device authentication. Information handled by Apple in those features is governed by Apple’s privacy policies.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may change when App features are added or updated. For important changes, we will notify you on this page or by another appropriate means.

9. Contact

For questions about the App or this Privacy Policy, please contact the support channel listed on the App Store.
